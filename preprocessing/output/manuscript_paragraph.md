Data treatment. During data preprocessing, we first examined the quality of the behavioral data at the participant level. Participants who left the browser window more than 1 time(s) were excluded (n = 2). Among the remaining participants, we further excluded those for whom more than 10% of trials had a reaction time outside the plausible range of 150-4000 ms (n = 2). From the remaining behavioral observations we omitted trials with a reaction time below 150 ms or above 4000 ms (1.67% of all remaining trials). This resulted in 1,180 trials across 6 participants (196.67 mean trials per participant).
